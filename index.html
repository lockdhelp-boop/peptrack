Common questions

A tracking tool,
not a medical app

What is PepTrack?

PepTrack is an iPhone app for organizing custom peptide routines, logging entries, setting reminders, and reviewing timing and history.

Does PepTrack provide medical or dosing advice?

No. PepTrack is a personal tracking and organizational tool. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend compounds, or provide dosing advice.

Does PepTrack require an account?

No. An account is not required to use the core tracking features.

Where is routine data stored?

Routine details, logs, schedules, reminders, notes, and custom entries are stored locally on your device. See the Privacy Policy for the limited account and analytics data handled by service providers.

Where can I learn more before downloading?

Visit the PepTrack guides for practical, non-medical information about organizing routines, reminders, and private tracking.
