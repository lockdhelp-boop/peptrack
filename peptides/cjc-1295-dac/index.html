What CJC-1295 with DAC's timing means for tracking

At roughly 8 days, CJC-1295 with DAC has the longest half-life of anything in PepTrack's catalogue — longer than semaglutide. Five half-lives puts clearance at around 40 days.

The DAC — Drug Affinity Complex — is the entire reason. It binds the molecule to serum albumin, which stops it being cleared at the rate the bare peptide would be. Same underlying GHRH analogue, radically different pharmacokinetics.

For a log this means two things. Levels accumulate over many weeks, so early entries and steady-state entries describe very different conditions even when identical. And a single missed entry stays visible in the curve for over a month. Date and interval are the whole story; clock time is irrelevant at this scale.

If you are comparing this against the no-DAC version, see CJC-1295 without DAC — same name in conversation, roughly 384 times the half-life here.

What to capture in the log

  • Record the date; the hour does not matter against a 192-hour half-life.
  • Expect the first several weeks to look unlike steady state. That is accumulation.
  • Note skipped entries — with a ~40-day clearance the gap remains visible for well over a month.

Worked reconstitution example

Purely as arithmetic, using round numbers rather than a recommendation. If a 10 mg vial were mixed with 2 mL of bacteriostatic water, the concentration would be 5 mg/mL. Measuring 2 mg from that would come to:

40 units on a U-100 syringe

10 mg ÷ 2 mL = 5 mg/mL  →  2 mg = 0.4 mL = 40 units

Change either the vial size or the water and this number changes completely. Run your own figures in the reconstitution calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is the half-life of CJC-1295 with DAC?

About 8 days, or roughly 192 hours, making it the longest-acting compound in PepTrack's catalogue. Clearance is near 40 days on the five-half-life convention.

What does DAC actually change?

The Drug Affinity Complex binds the peptide to serum albumin, which slows clearance enormously. Without it the same analogue has a half-life around 30 minutes.

How is CJC-1295 with DAC different from without?

Pharmacokinetically they are barely comparable: roughly 192 hours against roughly 0.5 hours. See the no-DAC page for that side.

How many units is 2 mg of CJC-1295 with DAC?

With a 10 mg vial in 2 mL the concentration is 5 mg/mL, so 2 mg is 0.4 mL — 40 units on a U-100 syringe.
