Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026  |  Contact: support.peptrack@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Tuned Tech LLC ("PepTrack," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the PepTrack mobile application, the pep-track.app website, advertising landing pages, web onboarding, Superwall web checkout, purchase-redemption flows, and related support services (together, the "Service").

PepTrack is a tracking and organizational tool, not a healthcare provider or medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, or dosing instructions.

Plain-language summary: Core tracking information such as compounds, schedules, dose logs, notes, reminders, and weight entries is stored locally on your device. Web questionnaire answers currently remain in your browser and are not sent to PepTrack, Meta, PostHog, Superwall, or Stripe. We store limited anonymous account, access, and creator-attribution records in Supabase; use PostHog for pseudonymous product analytics; use Meta tools for advertising measurement; and use Apple, Superwall, and Stripe to manage subscriptions and web checkout. Apple Health information is optional and is never used for advertising or analytics. We do not sell personal information or consumer health data.

1. Information Stored Locally in the App

The following information is stored in PepTrack's local app storage on your device:

  • Compounds you add or select
  • Dose logs, dates, amounts, and related entries
  • Protocol schedules and reminders
  • Personal notes and custom entries
  • Weight entries, goals, and display preferences
  • Other app settings and onboarding selections

PepTrack does not upload this core tracking information to our Supabase database. Deleting the app removes information held in PepTrack's local app storage from that device. Information separately written to Apple Health remains subject to your Apple Health settings and must be managed there.

2. Web Onboarding and Browser Storage

PepTrack's web onboarding asks questions that may include what you are tracking, how long you have been tracking it, your current tracking method, general challenges, goals, and an age range. These answers are processed temporarily in your browser to personalize the pages you see. PepTrack does not currently save or transmit those questionnaire answers. Refreshing or closing the page clears them.

The email field currently shown in web onboarding is disabled and does not collect or submit an email address.

Our advertising landing page temporarily stores the page's campaign query string and landing path in browser session storage. Campaign parameters such as source, campaign, advertisement, creator, angle, or funnel identifiers may be carried into the Superwall paywall so we can measure campaign performance. Reserved email and user-ID parameters are not forwarded by the PepTrack onboarding page. Session-storage values generally last until the browser tab or session ends.

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub and ordinary internet infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP address, browser or device type, requested page, referring page, and request time to deliver and protect the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Apple Health (HealthKit)

PepTrack optionally integrates with Apple Health after you grant permission. With permission, PepTrack can read body weight from Apple Health and write weights you enter in PepTrack back to Apple Health.

How we handle Apple Health information:

  • Apple Health access is optional and controlled through your device and Apple Health settings.
  • Apple Health information is not sent to Supabase, PostHog, Meta, Superwall, Stripe, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • Apple Health information is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or product analytics.
  • We do not sell Apple Health information.
  • You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings or the Apple Health app.

Deleting PepTrack does not necessarily delete information that was already written to Apple Health. You can view or delete those records and manage PepTrack's permissions in Apple Health.

4. Limited Account and Access Information

PepTrack uses Supabase to create and maintain a minimal anonymous app profile. Supabase may process:

  • A randomly generated account and profile identifier
  • An optional display name
  • Account creation and update timestamps
  • Onboarding-completion and paywall-reached status
  • Paid-access, entitlement-mirror, or administrator-approved bypass status
  • A creator or referral code redeemed in the app
  • Authentication and security information needed to maintain the anonymous session

Supabase does not receive your compound names, dose logs, protocol details, notes, weight values, Apple Health information, or web questionnaire answers from PepTrack. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.

5. Product Analytics (PostHog)

We use PostHog for pseudonymous product analytics so we can understand onboarding completion, feature use, reliability, and where users encounter problems. Analytics events are associated with a randomly generated analytics identifier rather than a name, email address, Apple ID, or payment card.

PostHog may receive:

  • App opens, lifecycle events, screen views, and navigation paths
  • Onboarding screens reached and steps completed
  • Notification-permission responses
  • The number of compounds selected, but not their names
  • The number of weight entries, but not weight values, dates, or goals
  • Creator or referral codes and whether a redemption was valid, invalid, bypassed, skipped, or unavailable
  • Paywall, access-gate, and technical error events
  • Basic app, operating-system, device, network, and event-delivery metadata

PepTrack does not intentionally send compound names, dose amounts, dose logs, schedules, notes, weight values, Apple Health information, web questionnaire answers, display names, email addresses, or payment information to PostHog. Session replay is not currently enabled in the PepTrack app. See the PostHog Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising and Meta Measurement

We use Meta advertising tools in separate parts of the Service to measure how advertising performs:

  • PepTrack landing-page Pixel: Our advertising landing page loads the Meta Pixel and sends a PageView event. Meta may receive the page URL and campaign parameters, referring page, browser and device information, IP-derived information, and Meta cookie or similar identifiers.
  • Superwall Web2App Pixel: Meta's browser Pixel inside Superwall may receive paywall views, checkout starts, completed purchases, paywall or account-management interactions, product and paywall identifiers, transaction or redemption interaction identifiers, price, and currency.
  • Meta iOS SDK: The PepTrack app uses Meta's iOS SDK for automatic app-event logging and advertising measurement. Meta may receive app-install, app-open, conversion, device, and technical signals, subject to Apple platform rules and your tracking permission where required.
  • Apple attribution: Apple privacy-preserving attribution technologies, including SKAdNetwork where applicable, may provide Meta with aggregate installation or conversion signals.

These sources may use the same Meta data source, and Meta may associate signals across browsers, devices, checkout, and the app under its own policies. PepTrack does not send web questionnaire answers, compound names, dose logs, protocol schedules, notes, weight values, or Apple Health information to Meta. We do not currently use Superwall's Meta Conversions API integration.

You can manage browser cookies through your browser and review iOS tracking permissions under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Blocking advertising technologies may reduce measurement but should not prevent you from using PepTrack's core tracking features. See Meta's Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy.

7. Subscriptions, Web Checkout, and Payments

PepTrack subscriptions may be purchased through Apple In-App Purchase or through Superwall Web Checkout powered by Stripe.

  • Apple purchases: Apple processes payment and manages the App Store subscription. PepTrack and Superwall receive subscription and entitlement status needed to provide premium access.
  • Web purchases: Superwall presents the web paywall, creates the Stripe checkout flow, manages purchase redemption, and connects an eligible purchase to PepTrack access.
  • Stripe: Stripe processes checkout information such as email address, payment method, billing details, transaction information, tax, fraud-prevention signals, and subscription records. For eligible Managed Payments transactions, Stripe may act as merchant of record.

Tuned Tech LLC does not receive or store your full payment-card number. We and our providers may receive or access checkout email, subscription status, product, price, currency, transaction identifiers, renewal or cancellation status, and related support information. Superwall may email purchase receipts, redemption links, and subscription-management links to the address used at checkout.

Resetting PepTrack, deleting the app, or requesting deletion of app data does not automatically cancel a subscription. Apple subscriptions must be managed through Apple, and web subscriptions must be managed through the Superwall or Stripe management flow provided with the purchase. See the Superwall Privacy Policy, Stripe Privacy Policy, and Apple Privacy Policy.

8. Support Communications

If you contact us, we process your email address and the information you choose to include so we can respond, troubleshoot, keep a record of the request, and protect the Service. Please do not include sensitive health information in a support request unless it is necessary for us to assist you.

9. Consumer Health Data Notice

Depending on where you live, information you enter about compounds, doses, schedules, weight, or related routines may be considered consumer health data even when it is not protected health information under HIPAA.

Categories and sources: PepTrack processes the tracking information you enter directly into the app, body-weight information you choose to read from or write to Apple Health, and questionnaire answers you enter into web onboarding.

Purposes: We process this information to provide the tracking, scheduling, reminder, calculator, chart, personalization, and Apple Health features you request.

Storage and sharing: Core app tracking information remains in local app storage, Apple Health information remains on your device and in Apple Health, and web questionnaire answers currently remain in browser memory. PepTrack does not share these contents with Supabase, PostHog, Meta, Superwall, Stripe, advertisers, or data brokers. Count-only product events, such as the number of compounds selected or number of weight entries, are not accompanied by compound names or weight values.

No sale or targeted advertising: We do not sell consumer health data or use its contents for targeted advertising. We do not permit third parties to use Apple Health information, tracking entries, or questionnaire answers for their own advertising.

You may exercise applicable consumer-health-data rights using the controls described below or by contacting support.peptrack@gmail.com.

11. Retention

  • Local app information remains until you delete individual entries, use available reset controls, or delete the app.
  • Apple Health information remains according to your Apple Health settings and deletion choices.
  • Web questionnaire answers last only in page memory; campaign parameters in session storage generally last for the browser session.
  • Supabase profile, access, and creator-attribution records remain as needed to operate access, prevent abuse, preserve attribution, and comply with deletion requests or legal requirements.
  • Analytics and advertising providers retain information according to our settings and their policies.
  • Purchase and transaction records may be retained for subscription management, receipts, accounting, tax, fraud prevention, disputes, refunds, and legal compliance.
  • Support communications are retained as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain appropriate records.

12. Your Choices, Reset, and Deletion

You can delete individual tracking entries in the app, revoke notification or tracking permissions in iOS Settings, manage Apple Health access and records in Apple Health, manage browser cookies through your browser, and export supported PepTrack data from the app.

Using PepTrack's in-app reset or deletion control clears local compounds, schedules, dose logs, reminders, display-name and onboarding selections, resets the current Superwall identity, resets portions of the anonymous profile state, and signs the current anonymous profile out. Weight entries can be managed separately in the app. This control does not cancel an Apple or web subscription, remove records held by Apple Health, or necessarily erase subscription, transaction, fraud-prevention, legal, or creator-attribution records.

Deleting the app removes its local app storage from that device but does not cancel a subscription or automatically remove Apple Health, payment-provider, analytics-provider, or server-held records.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of eligible server-held personal or consumer health information by emailing support.peptrack@gmail.com. We may need to verify enough information to locate the relevant anonymous profile or purchase. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and may retain information when reasonably required for billing, fraud prevention, security, legal obligations, or the establishment or defense of legal claims.

Local information is protected by your device's security, and service providers use their own security controls for information they process. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

PepTrack and its providers are based in or operate from the United States and other locations. Information may therefore be processed outside your state, province, or country under the safeguards and legal mechanisms used by the applicable provider.
